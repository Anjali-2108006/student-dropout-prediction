📢 Early Warning Overview & Support Guidance

The purpose of this dashboard is to help academic advisors and support staff identify students who may benefit from early intervention. Risk predictions are statistical estimates based on first-semester academic, demographic, and socio-economic indicators — they are not definitive assessments of a student's future.

🟢

Low Risk (< 30% probability)

These students show strong early retention indicators including consistent academic performance and financial stability.

Recommended Support: Continue regular academic monitoring and semester check-ins. Recognize and commend continued progress. Offer optional enrichment or mentoring opportunities.
🟡

Medium Risk (30–59% probability)

These students exhibit mixed indicators that may signal emerging academic challenges. Proactive engagement is encouraged before issues escalate.

Recommended Support: Consider an advisory check-in to discuss academic workload and study strategies. Explore tutoring resources, peer support groups, or adjusted coursework where applicable.
🔴

High Risk (≥ 60% probability)

These students show multiple indicators associated with higher dropout likelihood, including academic difficulties and financial pressures in their first semester.

Recommended Support: Timely academic advisor review is strongly recommended. Explore financial assistance options, counseling referrals, and flexible academic pathways. Support should be compassionate and constructive — not punitive.

🛡️ Important Limitations & Ethical Notice

  • Dropout risk predictions are probabilistic estimates, not certainties. The model has an accuracy of approximately 84% — meaning some predictions will be incorrect.
  • This tool does not identify the specific reason a student may drop out. Many factors affecting a student's decision are beyond the data available to this model.
  • Predictions should never be used to automatically label students as failures or to justify punitive, exclusionary, or disciplinary actions.
  • All predictions should be reviewed by authorized academic staff before any action is taken. Human judgment remains essential.
  • Student records in this dashboard are fully anonymized — no names, contact details, or personal identifiers are stored or displayed.
