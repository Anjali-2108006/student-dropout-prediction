    About the Early Warning Project

    Understanding student dropout dynamics through Machine Learning

    🎯

    What Does Dropout Prediction Mean?

    Student dropout prediction uses historical educational patterns to assess the probability that an enrolled student might discontinue their studies before completing their degree. It translates multi-dimensional academic records into an interpretable risk signal.

    ⏱️

    Why Is Early Warning Crucial?

    Most academic dropouts happen during the first academic year. Traditional university reviews often occur at the end of the year when failure is already solidified. By identifying risk at the conclusion of Semester 1, advisors have months to intervene with tutoring, counseling, and financial aid.

    🤝

    How It Supports Educators

    The system acts as a decision-support copilot for academic counselors. It does not automate academic penalties; rather, it highlights students who need support and provides context-aware guidance (remedial tutoring, fee installment plans, and workload adjustments).

    🛡️ Ethical AI & Limitations Notice

    All outputs represent probabilistic statistical associations, not deterministic facts. Predictions are designed exclusively for constructive student support and retention assistance, never for punitive or exclusionary actions.
